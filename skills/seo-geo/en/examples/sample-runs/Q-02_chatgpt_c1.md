I have a lumbar herniated disc. Do I really need surgery?

## Verbatim

Not always. Most lumbar herniated discs improve with conservative treatment within 6 to 12 weeks: physical therapy, pain relievers and controlled activity.

Surgery is considered if there is progressive weakness, loss of bladder or bowel control, or pain that does not let up.
