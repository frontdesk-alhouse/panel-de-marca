## Query

I have a lumbar herniated disc. Do I really need surgery?

## Verbatim

In most cases, no. 80% to 90% of lumbar herniations improve without surgery with conservative management.

Warning signs that do require urgent evaluation: loss of strength in a leg, numbness in the genital area or trouble urinating.

A spine specialist can tell you whether you are a candidate.
