# Backlinks: audit approach

> Ported and adapted from concepts in `AgriciDaniel/claude-seo` (v2.2.4, MIT). Backlinks still
> carry weight, but in 2026 the signal is **relevance and earned authority**, not volume.

---

## Principle

One link from a relevant site with real authority is worth more than a hundred from junk
directories. Google penalizes artificial link schemes. The sustainable strategy is to **earn
mentions**, not buy them.

## What to audit

1. **Current profile**: how many referring domains, quality, topical relevance, anchor text
   (too much exact-match = red flag).
2. **Toxic links**: spam domains pointing at the site → consider a disavow only if there is
   clear harm.
3. **Unlinked mentions**: brands that name you without linking → an opportunity to recover the
   link.
4. **Competitor backlinks**: where relevant competitors get their links → a map of realistic
   opportunities.

## How to earn links (sustainably)

- Genuinely citable content (your own data, definitive guides, case studies).
- Digital PR and industry press.
- Legitimate, relevant directories (for local: GBP, chambers of commerce, associations).
- Real collaborations, not link farms.

## Tools

- **Search Console → Links**: the free links report, straight from the source (Google).
- Paid APIs (**optional, future phase, with your own keys**): DataForSEO, Ahrefs, SE Ranking;
  the claude-seo plugin supports them as extensions if the cost is ever justified. They are not
  needed for the core.

> Do not promise "X backlinks per month" as a deliverable. Promise a clean link profile + a
> citable content strategy.
